# Open-source and published gold (XAUUSD) / forex intraday scalping bots and EAs — inventory of rules, results, and verification status

Labels used throughout: **VERIFIED** = Myfxbook / FX Blue / MQL5-signal live account or genuine out-of-sample test with stated method; **BACKTEST-ONLY** = Strategy Tester / Python backtest with no live record; **MARKETING CLAIM** = vendor or reseller figures with no checkable record. Note: a Myfxbook "verified" badge only confirms the account history is linked; it does not audit the EA, the broker, or whether the account is demo/real/cent.

Research-access note: forexfactory.com and myfxbook.com community threads returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so Forex Factory / Myfxbook thread content below comes from search snippets only and is flagged as such.

## Key question 1 — Which specific repos/EAs/strategies for gold M1–M15 scalping have code or full rules public, and what are the rules in detail?

### Takeaway
Roughly a dozen public repos/articles expose full or partial rules for XAUUSD M5/M15 scalpers; the recurring open-source designs are (a) EMA-trend + RSI/stochastic pullback on M5, (b) ICT/FVG session-window scalpers, (c) NY/London opening-range breakout, and (d) martingale/grid "scalpers". None of the fully open-source ones has a verified live track record; the only ones with live Myfxbook/MQL5-signal records are closed-source commercial EAs whose entry logic is only described in prose.

### Cited Findings

**Open-source MQL5 / Python with full or partial rules**

- **mad808/gold-scalper-mt5 ("Gold Sniper Scalper" v16.30, MQL5, XAUUSD M5)** — martingale grid. Trend filter: EMA 13/34 on M5 plus EMA 200 on H1; entry when RSI reaches oversold/overbought "only if" trend matches; `UseDynamicRsi = True` shifts RSI limits by ADX (more aggressive in ranges). Exits: basket take-profit when weighted-average profit reaches default **40 points**; initial entry split into 3 positions with staged TPs; "smart trailing" TP; no break-even. Sizing/grid: `AutoLot=True`, `AutoLotStep=$60` (0.01 lot per $60), `MaxGridLevels=8`, `LotMultiplier=1.6`, ATR-based grid spacing (period/multiplier not given). Protection: `BasketSL_USD=$5.00`, `MaxDrawdown%=95.0`. Filters: optional London/NY session restriction, optional high-impact-news pause, no spread filter. Reported results: **none**; README says the martingale "can result in the total loss of your deposit" and the project is "for educational and analytical purposes only" — [GitHub mad808/gold-scalper-mt5](https://github.com/mad808/gold-scalper-mt5)
- **BAKOME-Hub/BAKOMEGoldScalper (MQL5, MIT, XAUUSD M5)** — ICT-style. Entry: Fair Value Gaps, Order Blocks, liquidity sweeps, aligned with EMA bias on H1/H4 (periods not stated); "Silver Bullet" windows London 08:00–09:00 and New York 15:00–16:00 broker time (`UseOrderBlocks`, `UseFairValueGaps`, `UseSilverBullet` default on). Exits: ATR-based SL/TP (multiples not stated), `UseTrailingStop` on with `Trail_StartATR=1.5`, break-even and optional partial close (no parameters). Risk: `RiskPercent=1.0`, `MaxDailyRiskPercent=5.0`, `MaxDailyTrades=10`, lot from risk and ATR. Filters: `MaxSpreadPoints=50`, `MinATR_Points=100`, London+NY sessions (Asia optional), no news filter. Exact entry conditions live only in the .mq5 source — [GitHub BAKOME-Hub/BAKOMEGoldScalper](https://github.com/BAKOME-Hub/BAKOMEGoldScalper)
- **Sandyyy123/xauusd-scalper-research (Python backtest + MQL5 export, XAUUSD M5)** — trend-pullback. Trend: EMA 20/50 crossover, trade only in trend direction; trigger: RSI(7) divergence plus Stochastic(5,3,3) "to pinpoint reversals within trend"; volatility gate ATR(14) threshold (value not given); session: London–NY overlap 13:00–17:00 UTC favoured, Asia excluded "for high spread and range-bound conditions"; SL = 1.0×ATR(14) from entry candle, TP = 1.5×ATR(14) (1:1.5 R:R); max 2 concurrent positions; no martingale. ### Inferences
- The only repos that publish both code and parameter values are the martingale grid (mad808) and the ICT scalper (BAKOME); the trend-pullback research repo (Sandyyy123) publishes rules but withholds the ATR gate value and cost model.
- The open-source gold "scalpers" cluster into four templates: EMA-trend + oscillator pullback on M5; ICT/FVG in fixed session windows; opening-range breakout (NY or Asian range); and martingale/grid with tiny basket TP. Everything with a public .mq5 file is one of these.
- Several commercial listings describe exactly the same "H1/H4 EMA bias + M5 EMA20 pullback" concept as the open repos, so the concept is commodity; the differentiator vendors sell is the (undisclosed) filters and exits.

- Exact EMA periods / RSI thresholds for the maskchanel14 EA and the exact ATR SL/TP multiples for BAKOME are only in source files not readable via the PR/README pages.

## Key question 3 — What entry concept recurs among the ones with credible evidence?

### Takeaway
Among sources with any credible (out-of-sample or live) evidence, the recurring entries are session-anchored breakouts (NY opening range, London/NY windows) and trend-aligned pullbacks (H1/H4 EMA bias + M5 EMA20/50 pullback); indicator-stacked M15 confluence and pure oscillator mean reversion tested poorly in the one grid-search comparison found.

- The one multi-strategy grid search found (gold, 15 archetypes, 70/30 OOS, friction modelled) ranked opening-range breakout with EMA50 trend filter best (PF 1.91 / OOS 2.71) and ranked pullback-to-EMA21 (PF 0.72), MACD zero-cross (0.93), Donchian-20 breakout (0.89), RSI 20/80 mean reversion (~1.0) and indicator-stacked M15 confluence (0.72) as losing or flat — [dev.to alallaqi](https://dev.to/alallaqi/i-built-a-gold-trading-bot-from-scratch-heres-what-actually-works-and-what-destroyed-my-account-3pij)

### Inferences
- The evidence base for M1–M15 gold scalping entries is thin: the best-documented out-of-sample result is an H1 session-breakout system, not a scalper, and the most common open-source scalper concept (EMA pullback + oscillator) has one negative 21-year result and one unverifiable positive backtest.
- Concepts that recur with at least weak evidence: (1) NY/London session opening-range breakout with HTF trend filter and volatility stop; (2) H1/H4-trend + M5 EMA20 pullback restricted to the London–NY overlap. Asia-session mean reversion appears only as a research hypothesis (soloshun) and as a reason to exclude Asia (Sandyyy123), not as a tested edge.

### Gaps
- No source compares entry concepts on M1–M15 gold with modelled spread/commission out of sample; the only comparison (dev.to) uses hourly bars and futures data.
- No independent evidence was found for VWAP-based gold scalpers.

## Key question 4 — What exit structure recurs, and do fixed-small-TP / larger-SL (high win rate, negative R:R) strategies survive live?

### Takeaway
Credible sources use ATR-based stops (1–1.5×ATR) with targets ≥ 1.5R and trailing/time exits; the one controlled experiment found on gold shows that shrinking TP to 0.2–0.5R against 5–6×ATR stops raises win rate to 68–87% while profit factor stays ~1.0 (zero net), and multiple sources link the high-win-rate/small-TP profile to hidden losses and live failure via slippage.

### Cited Findings
- Controlled TP/SL sweep on XAUUSD 2023–2026 (entry unchanged, Strategy Tester; author sells an EA): TP 0.2R / SL 6×ATR → win rate 87.1%, PF 1.00, net ~zero; TP 0.3R / SL 5×ATR → win rate 81%, PF 1.10; TP 0.5R → win rate 68%, PF 1.00; author: "A high win rate on gold is not evidence of an edge. It is a property of where you place the take-profit relative to the stop." ### Inferences
- No source in this inventory shows a negative-R:R, high-win-rate gold scalper surviving live over a year with a verified account; the sources that address the profile directly (TP/SL sweep, 95%-WR article, slippage case) all describe it as break-even-at-best before costs and fragile after costs.
- Early break-even and 1R partial exits were explicitly blamed for losses in two independent backtests, suggesting that on gold these "protection" layers truncate the right tail that pays for the stops.
- The exit structure shared by the sources with the best evidence is: volatility stop (1–1.5×ATR), target ≥ 1.5–2R or trailing after ≥ 1.5×ATR, no averaging, one or two positions.

### Gaps
- No verified live statement was found for a small-TP/large-SL gold scalper, so "do they survive live" is answered only indirectly (backtest sweeps and anecdotes), not with a long live record.
- The TP/SL sweep does not report trade counts or drawdowns per configuration.

## Key question 5 — Why do gold scalping bots fail live, per credible community sources?

### Takeaway
Sources converge on five mechanisms: live spread/commission/slippage erasing a thin per-trade edge (gold spreads 10–30× idealised test spread), tester artefacts (1-min OHLC, fixed spread), martingale/grid structures that die on one trend day (e.g. the 30 Jan 2026 −9.5% day), regime changes after an optimised window, and news spikes/widened spreads at the exact session windows scalpers trade.

### Cited Findings
- Gold Strike review: backtest "ran with an idealised 1-point spread"; "Real gold spreads are 10-30x higher"; EA's MaxSpread 20 points while standard brokers quote "30-50+ points" so it "may never trade on them"; PF drop ~75% from backtest to live attributed to spreads, commissions, slippage; ECN/RAW + VPS "mandatory" — [ForexCracked](https://www.forexcracked.com/forex-ea/forexcracked-gold-strike-ea-free-download-xauusd-breakout-scalper-review/)
- Live slippage averaged 0.7–0.8 pips against a 0.5-pip expectancy for a scalping EA — [myforexvps](https://myforexvps.com/why-forex-eas-fail-in-live-trading/)

### Inferences
- For a bot targeting tens of points on gold, a live spread of 12–50 points plus 0.7–0.8-pip average slippage is on the order of the whole expected edge, which is why the sources see win rates survive but profit factors collapse (6.75 → 1.66 in the one example with both numbers).
- Grid/martingale failure on gold is not a tail event: by the Jan-2026 model, a 1.5× multiplier dies on a 2.3–4% move, and gold regularly moves that much intraday, so any gold grid should be assumed to have a finite life measured in months.
- Because the credible session windows (London/NY opens, 13:00–17:00 UTC) coincide with US data releases, news filters are a necessity rather than an optional add-on for gold scalpers; most open-source repos either omit them (BAKOME, Sandyyy123) or leave them optional (mad808).

### Gaps
- No source gives measured live slippage in points specifically for XAUUSD at a named broker; the 0.7–0.8-pip figure is a generic forex scalper case.
- No real blown-account statement with dates and drawdown was found for a specific gold grid EA; the Jan-2026 analysis is a model and the forum reports are unquantified.
- Rollover/swap and weekend-gap effects on gold scalpers were not covered by any source retrieved.
